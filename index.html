About Me

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Chemical Engineering at King Fahd University of Petroleum & Minerals (KFUPM). My research focuses on the development of advanced materials and chemical engineering solutions for environmental and energy-related applications.

My research experience spans the engineering of multicomponent adsorbents, graphene-based materials, layered hydroxides, and zeolitic imidazolate frameworks (ZIFs) for wastewater treatment and H₂S scavenging. I also work on the formulation and optimization of water- and oil-based drilling fluid systems and the investigation of complex fluid behavior.

An important aspect of my work is connecting experimental chemical engineering with computational forecasting. I have experience applying hybrid artificial intelligence and ensemble machine-learning models to adsorption systems to improve prediction and process optimization.

Research Interests

Advanced Nanomaterials

Design, synthesis, functionalization, and characterization of multifunctional nanomaterials including graphene-based composites, layered hydroxides, and ZIF materials.

Wastewater Treatment

Development of advanced adsorbents for removing dyes, phenolic compounds, heavy metals, and other organic and inorganic pollutants from contaminated water.

Drilling Fluids & H₂S Scavenging

Formulation and optimization of water- and oil-based drilling fluids, with emphasis on rheological performance, operational stability, and hydrogen sulfide removal.

Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

Application of hybrid AI and ensemble machine-learning methods for forecasting adsorption performance and supporting chemical process optimization.

Adsorption & Environmental Remediation

Adsorption thermodynamics, material-performance relationships, solution chemistry, and development of sustainable environmental remediation technologies.

Complex Fluid Systems

Rheology, emulsions, biosurfactants, crude-oil/water nanoemulsions, and stimulus-responsive fluid systems.
